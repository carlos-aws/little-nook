# Credits and licenses

Little Nook's code, illustrations, character, questions, and synthesized audio
are original work for this project and distributed under the MIT license.

The graphics take broad inspiration from the cozy outlined objects and warm,
domestic settings in Meox Studio's *Organized Inside*. No graphics, characters,
audio, game text, or other assets from that game are included.

The care-through-learning concept is informed by the sibling Pixel Pals
project. Its JavaScript, pixel art, and AWS infrastructure are not dependencies
of this game.
